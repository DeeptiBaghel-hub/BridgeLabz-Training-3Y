Typography and Text Styling

A Short Article on Typography

Typography is the art of arranging text so that it is easy to read and pleasant to look at. Good typography uses the right font family, the right size, and the right spacing between lines and letters.

This paragraph uses a different font family and weight to show how text can look completely different depending on the styling choices. Notice the letter-spacing and line-height applied here as well, which change how comfortable the text is to read.
